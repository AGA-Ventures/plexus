# Design System: Plexus

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Governed Blue Agenda"**

Plexus is a calm blue operating layer where cross-border business intent becomes a human-reviewed path to a real meeting and accountable follow-up. Its public world is editorial and assured: saturated blue fields, a midnight logo bay, mineral paper, photographed working context, unified product-system imagery, and tangible agenda fragments explain the journey before they ask for action. The exact Plexus mark remains the identity anchor; it is never redrawn, recolored, or replaced by an improvised symbol.

Authenticated workspaces translate that agenda into an operating system. The typography becomes compact, the canvas shifts to pale mineral blue, and information is grouped with flat cards, hairlines, explicit statuses, and a midnight navigation rail. Connection Blue, cyan, imagery, and motion provide orientation, while product claims, provider readiness, sensitive decisions, and outcomes stay visibly human-governed and evidence-bound.

Authentication is a scoped atmospheric bridge and may place a glass panel over the Plexus network artwork. The public pre-event service is a second scoped expression: it uses the shared Midnight, Filled Surface Blue, Connection Blue, cyan, mineral, and pale-network palette with tighter campaign geometry and denser service content.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Saturated production-blue fields anchored by midnight and mineral paper.
- Editorial public scale translated into compact operational hierarchy.
- Pale network grouping and cyan signals that clarify a governed journey.
- Flat, scan-first workspaces with explicit status and human review.
- Pre-launch and capability-readiness qualification placed beside the visual evidence.
- The exact Plexus mark, Inter typography, and restrained Geist Mono metadata.
- Responsive stacking, mobile navigation sheets, and reduced-motion parity.

## Colors

The palette is a blue editorial system: deep filled surfaces carry action and structure, brighter blue and cyan carry signal, and mineral neutrals keep public and operational information readable.

### Primary

- **Filled Surface Blue** (`colors.filled-surface-blue`): The normative production blue for small-text filled calls-to-action, selected navigation, checked controls, and substantial branded panels. Its deeper hover step is `colors.filled-surface-blue-hover`.
- **Midnight** (`colors.midnight`): The navigation rail, public dark section, hero action, and secure entry foundation. `colors.midnight-raised` is its observed hover or raised-panel step.

### Secondary

- **Connection Blue** (`colors.connection-blue`): The bright focus, caret, selection, progress-track, outline, and ambient-field value. It may fill a large authentication action with dark text, but it is not the default small-text filled CTA.
- **Network Cyan** (`colors.network-cyan`): The brighter technical signal used by the product preview and network-oriented accents.
- **Signal Cyan** (`colors.signal-cyan`): The softer cyan for status dots, icons, quiet links, and legible signals on Midnight.

### Tertiary

- **Live Readiness** (`colors.readiness-live-surface` with `colors.readiness-live-ink`): Persisted and verified in the controlled product.
- **Mixed Readiness** (`colors.readiness-mixed-surface` with `colors.filled-surface-blue`): A live workflow with a controlled or adapter step.
- **Adapter Readiness** (`colors.readiness-adapter-surface` with `colors.readiness-adapter-ink`): The interface exists while its production provider remains incomplete.
- **Concept Readiness** (`colors.readiness-concept-surface` with `colors.readiness-concept-ink`): Illustrative direction that is not live.

These paired styles qualify product-preview evidence only. They communicate implementation readiness when accompanied by their written labels and shared definitions, not generic success, warning, or category meaning.

### Neutral

- **Mineral Paper** (`colors.mineral-paper`): The warm public canvas and light agenda-card surface.
- **Operational Canvas** (`colors.operational-canvas`): The cool workspace background behind operational cards and rails.
- **Card Surface** (`colors.card-surface`): The near-white operational card and popover plane.
- **Primary Ink** (`colors.primary-ink`): The default text color on light surfaces.
- **Muted Ink** (`colors.muted-ink`): Supporting descriptions, metadata, and lower-emphasis operational copy.
- **Pale Network** (`colors.pale-network`): Journey tracks, selected-neutral groups, informative panels, and mobile menu emphasis.
- **Hairline** (`colors.hairline`): Operational borders, field strokes, table dividers, and card rings.
- **White** (`colors.white`): Text on filled surfaces, light public actions, and high-contrast auth content.

### Named Rules

**The Filled Surface Rule.** Small-text filled actions and active navigation use Filled Surface Blue (`#0758c8`), never the brighter Connection Blue.

**The Signal Rule.** Connection Blue and the two cyan values indicate focus, progress, network activity, or ambient energy; they do not independently mean approved, compliant, ready, or successful.

**The Campaign Cohesion Rule.** The pre-event route uses the shared blue palette while retaining its compact 4px controls and service-led density. Signal Cyan may orient visitors on Midnight, but Filled Surface Blue remains the small-text action color.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Inter (with sans-serif fallback)

**Body Font:** Inter (with sans-serif fallback)

**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with monospace fallback)

**Character:** Inter gives Plexus a direct, multilingual neo-grotesk voice that survives both editorial scale and dense operating work. Geist Mono is a restrained instrument for technical signals, product-preview metadata, identifiers, and short stage labels—not a second display personality.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (600, `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5.75rem)`, 0.94): Short public promises and major journey statements with tightly controlled line breaks.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(1.875rem, 4vw, 3rem)`, 1.08): Workspace page titles, major operating sections, and compact campaign statements.
- **Title** (600, `1.25rem`, 1.25): Card titles, modal titles, summary figures, and route choices.
- **Body** (400, `1rem`, 1.5): Public explanation and longer operational guidance; keep sustained text near 65–75 characters where layout permits.
- **Label** (500, `0.75rem`, 1.625): Buttons, navigation, field labels, tables, and compact metadata.
- **Readiness** (750, `0.6875rem`, 1.45): The written Live, Mixed, Adapter, and Concept labels beside product-preview evidence.
- **Mono** (600, `0.6875rem`, 1.5): Product-preview signals and terse technical metadata, usually with modest tracking.

### Named Rules

**The One-Family Rule.** Inter carries the main Plexus voice across public, authenticated, and localized surfaces; do not introduce a decorative display face.

**The Metadata Rule.** Uppercase tracking and Geist Mono are reserved for short orientation or technical metadata; they are not the default preheading treatment for every section.

## Layout

Public pages use a fluid container up to 1440px with 16px mobile gutters, 24px small-screen gutters, and 32px large-screen gutters. Editorial heroes pair decisive copy with photographed working context or a midnight proof panel at the large breakpoint, then stack into one readable column on smaller screens. The public journey uses large section spacing and converts multi-column rails into bordered vertical sequences without compressing copy.

The product-preview hero is one Filled Surface Blue field that pairs a concise superapp promise with one unified product-system composite and places the five-step journey rail immediately below it. On mobile, the first viewport stacks the promise, a full-width primary action, the explicit availability note, and the pre-launch-qualified product image before the single-column journey rail; the media may bleed to the viewport edge while its qualification remains attached.

Authenticated Admin and Vendor pages use the same 1440px operating frame; Superadmin may extend to 1500px. At 1024px and above, a sticky 220px midnight rail sits beside a fluid content column. Below that breakpoint, the rail becomes a left-side sheet launched from a sticky midnight mobile bar. Summary metrics move from a two-column mobile grid to four columns at 1280px, and dense tables switch to stacked record cards below 768px.

Authentication is capped near 1180px and becomes a brand-and-form split at 768px. Public and operational form actions stack before there is enough room for a horizontal row. Every layout must tolerate the supported English, Chinese, and Thai product locales, tenant identity, and longer translated actions without hiding the responsible next step.

### Named Rules

**The Sheet-not-Squeeze Rule.** Mobile navigation opens as a left sheet; never compress the desktop rail into an unreadable strip.

**The Record-not-Scroll Rule.** Dense operational tables become stacked record cards on narrow screens instead of depending on horizontal page scrolling.

**The Preview First-Viewport Rule.** On mobile product previews, keep the promise, full-width primary action, pre-launch qualification, and unified product image in that order before the journey rail; do not lead with a detached feature card or hide qualification below the first product view.

## Elevation & Depth

Plexus uses tonal layering first. Operational cards are flat near-white surfaces on the cool canvas, bounded by a one-pixel ring or hairline; elevation is reserved for sticky navigation, menus, dialogs, mobile sheets, and explicitly floating public fragments. Public imagery may sit in a deep editorial frame, but glow is ambient rather than semantic. Authentication alone uses strong blur, translucency, and depth as a deliberate material exception.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Operational Rail** (`0 18px 42px rgba(7, 19, 38, 0.12)`): Sticky desktop navigation that must remain distinct from the workspace canvas.
- **Editorial Frame** (`0 24px 70px rgba(7, 19, 38, 0.2)`): The saturated public hero frame over the bright ambient field.
- **Agenda Fragment** (`0 12px 28px rgba(7, 19, 38, 0.18)`): Floating proof cards on public photography; the dark fragment may deepen to 0.3 opacity.
- **Mobile Menu** (`0 20px 55px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.28)`): The public mobile menu over Midnight.
- **Authentication Glass** (`0 30px 90px rgba(1, 5, 24, 0.52)`): Sign-in and recovery panels over the Plexus network artwork only.

### Named Rules

**The Flat-by-Default Rule.** If surface tone, spacing, and a hairline already establish hierarchy, do not add a shadow.

**The Scoped Glass Rule.** Backdrop blur and translucent glass belong to authentication and temporary overlays, not routine operational cards.

## Shapes

The shared interface uses a measured curve derived from a 14px base: compact controls resolve near 11px, standard cards at 14px, grouped panels near 20–25px, and the public editorial frame at 22px. Pills are reserved for status, language, and short categorical controls. Authentication may reach 26.4px; the pre-event campaign deliberately tightens key actions to 4px. Borders remain one pixel unless focus or validation needs a stronger ring.

### Named Rules

**The Curve by Scale Rule.** Controls are tighter than cards, cards are tighter than editorial frames, and only auth glass earns the largest system radius.

## Components

Components are crisp, content-led, and explicit about state. Operational variants are compact; public and authentication actions increase target height without changing the underlying focus and contrast contract.

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Operational buttons use the medium control radius (`11.2px`) at a default height of 28px; public and auth actions use 48px targets with 14px or 12px corners.
- **Primary:** The compact filled action is Filled Surface Blue with white text; the observed hover is Filled Surface Blue Hover. Public blue panels use a Midnight primary action and a white secondary action.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover changes tone or opacity with restrained motion. Keyboard focus uses the global two-pixel Connection Blue outline with a 3px offset; dark campaign and auth controls may substitute a visible white or Signal Cyan ring. The product-preview route uses a dual ring—a 3px white outline at a 3px offset plus a 6px Filled Surface Blue outer ring—so focus remains visible across both light and dark preview surfaces.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outline and ghost actions stay transparent or lightly tinted until hover. Active operational press feedback moves down one pixel when the control does not open a popup.

### Chips

- **Style:** Status chips are compact pills with 10–12px readable text, an explicit text label, and either a filled semantic tone or pale tonal background.
- **State:** Color always accompanies text or an icon; a cyan dot may show live or in-progress activity, but never substitutes for the written state.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** Standard operational cards use the 14px surface radius; editorial journey groups use the 20–25px range.
- **Background:** Near-white cards sit on Operational Canvas; Pale Network groups related steps; blue and Midnight metric blocks are used sparingly to establish rhythm.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Cards remain flat and use a one-pixel ring. Only public agenda fragments, sticky rails, and overlays use the named shadows.
- **Internal Padding:** Compact cards use 16px; major grouped regions use 20–28px.

### Inputs / Fields

- **Style:** Operational fields are 28px high with a subtle cool fill, one-pixel input border, compact padding, and the medium control radius. Authentication fields expand to 48px with a translucent white fill and 12px corners.
- **Focus:** Focus shifts the border to Connection Blue and adds a visible two-pixel ring; the text caret also uses Connection Blue.
- **Error / Disabled:** Errors use both destructive text and a ring or alert treatment. Disabled controls remain legible, lower opacity, and cannot imply that an unavailable provider or permission is active.

### Navigation

- **Desktop:** A Midnight rail contains full-width 40px destinations. The active item is Filled Surface Blue with white text; inactive items use pale text and a dark tonal hover.
- **Mobile:** A sticky Midnight bar names the current section and opens a left sheet with the same route order, selected state, account context, and logout behavior.
- **Public:** The exact Plexus wordmark sits in a Midnight header. Active links invert to white; the filled login action uses Filled Surface Blue. Public language choices collapse into one visible current-locale control and open as a compact menu on click.

### Product Preview Hero

The product-preview hero presents the whole business journey as one unified superapp composite rather than a collage of detached feature cards. A visible “Pre-launch product preview” badge stays attached to the media, while the availability sentence sits beside the primary action. Desktop composition keeps the promise and image in one Filled Surface Blue field; mobile preserves both qualifications in the first-view sequence before the journey rail.

### Readiness Labels

Product-preview evidence uses a shared four-state vocabulary: **Live** means persisted and verified in the controlled product; **Mixed** means a live workflow includes a controlled or adapter step; **Adapter** means the interface exists but the production provider is incomplete; **Concept** means illustrative direction that is not live. Every label remains textual and appears with the shared legend; its color never carries readiness alone.

### Program Pulse

The program pulse is the signature operating component: a Midnight strip that names the governed five-stage journey and places a slim Connection Blue indicator beneath the current stage. It provides orientation, not automated scoring, and stage copy remains explicit.

### Authentication Glass

The auth panel is a 26.4px translucent surface with a white hairline, 32px backdrop blur, and the Authentication Glass shadow. Tenant accent values may style the 48px primary button only when foreground contrast remains readable. The Plexus network image and exact tenant or Plexus mark remain visible context, never decoration inside operational pages.

### Motion

Route arrival uses 420ms and hero reveal uses 800ms with `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; the program progress track uses 1000ms with the same easing. Hover and component-state feedback stays near 180–200ms. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, these animations stop, smooth scrolling is disabled, and state changes remain immediately understandable.

### Named Rules

**The Same State Rule.** Desktop rail, mobile sheet, table card, dialog, and localized variant must expose the same route, status, and next action.

**The Human-Governed Rule.** Components may assist matching, preparation, and follow-up, but visual polish never turns a planned adapter, sensitive decision, or unverified outcome into a production claim.

**The Qualification-at-the-Point Rule.** Every illustrative product treatment carries its qualification beside the visual or module copy, and every readiness label appears with the shared legend; a page-level disclaimer alone is insufficient.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use Filled Surface Blue (`#0758c8`) for compact filled actions and active destinations.
- **Do** keep Connection Blue (`#0a84ff`) for focus, progress, selection, large ambient fields, and other high-visibility signals.
- **Do** pair every status color with readable text or an icon and preserve explicit human review.
- **Do** preserve the exact Plexus wordmark asset and approved tenant identity without weakening contrast.
- **Do** use mineral canvases, Pale Network grouping, and hairlines before reaching for elevation.
- **Do** verify mobile sheet navigation, stacked records, longer translated labels, keyboard focus, and reduced motion.
- **Do** keep authentication glass scoped to secure entry and preserve the pre-event route's compact campaign geometry within the shared blue palette.
- **Do** place pre-launch, illustrative, and readiness qualification beside the relevant product image or module copy.

### Don't:

- **Don't** use Connection Blue as the filled background behind small white CTA text.
- **Don't** revive Harbor Teal, emerald, or lime as competing campaign accents inside the shared blue editorial system.
- **Don't** float every operational card or apply glass and glow to routine workspace content.
- **Don't** compress desktop rails or dense tables into unreadable mobile layouts.
- **Don't** redraw, recolor, crop into a new symbol, or substitute the Plexus mark.
- **Don't** use animation, color, badges, or polished imagery as evidence of approval, compliance, provider readiness, market coverage, or business outcomes.
- **Don't** reuse Live, Mixed, Adapter, and Concept colors as generic success, warning, or category styling.

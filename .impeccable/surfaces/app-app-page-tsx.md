# Product Preview Superapp Brief

Scope: Public English-only `/app` product-preview route; mode: Persuade and
explain.

Audience and job: Program operators and participating companies need to see
how the complete governed business journey fits together, identify the modules
relevant to them, and distinguish live product behavior from provider adapters
and future concepts.

Primary action and proof: “Explore the connected product” leads into a
profile → match → meeting → agreement → follow-up story. Real product truth is
shown through the maintained twelve-domain capability map; illustrative device

showcase. A saturated Filled Surface Blue hero carries one unified mobile and
desktop ecosystem; mineral and pale-network sections alternate with Midnight

imagery create the premium product-design level.

promise with the connected three-device Plexus system, followed by a five-step
journey rail.

Constraints: Preserve the exact Plexus wordmark, the English-only route

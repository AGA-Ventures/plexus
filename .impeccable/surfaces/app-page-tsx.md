Scope: Public pre-launch website routes rooted at `app/page.tsx`; mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: Tenant operators must understand that Plexus is a multilingual, white-label, human-governed operating layer and decide to request early access. Participating businesses need a clear route to the business journey and controlled login.

Primary action and proof: “White-label with Plexus” leads to contact; “Join as a business” leads to the business route. The page demonstrates profile → organizer review → mutual acceptance → confirmed meeting → agreement/follow-up as one governed itinerary that identifies the decision owner and recorded outcome at each handoff, with no fabricated proof.

The homepage hero uses a purpose-built governed-collaboration scene rather than a generic office or travel-planning photograph: the program operator, business owner, shared laptop, human-review state, and four-stage workflow remain legible behind the live three-stage rail.

Constraints: Preserve the exact Plexus logo and query-param locale behavior. Label the product pre-launch. Keep `/pre-event` as a distinct campaign exception. Avoid autonomous, provider-readiness, customer, market, pricing, or outcome claims without evidence.

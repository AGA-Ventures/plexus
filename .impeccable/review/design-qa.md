## Coverage checked

- The first viewport identifies Plexus as one governed business superapp,
  preserves the exact wordmark, exposes a primary action, and presents a
  unified mobile/desktop product ecosystem.
- The journey rail exposes profile, match, meeting, agreement, and follow-up
  without requiring inference from imagery.
- Company foundation, governed matching, meetings, interpretation, meeting
  prompts, action briefs, agreements, PLEXA, and all twelve product domains are
  findable in the page.
- Live, mixed, adapter, and concept readiness are paired with text and never
  rely on color alone.
- Desktop, mobile, and the user's 1327-pixel viewport have no horizontal
  document overflow.
- The primary hero link resolves to `#experience`; section links retain native
  keyboard and hash behavior.
- Reduced-motion handling, focus rings, text selection, responsive image
  sizing, and readable mobile stacking are present.

## Detector notes

The Impeccable detector returned advisory-only design-system observations.
Mechanical font-ramp, radius, and palette drifts were aligned where they did
not weaken the approved composition. Remaining advisories cover intentionally
semantic readiness colors and route-specific tonal values documented by the
surface brief; no blocker-level anti-pattern was reported.
